7 solution-context interview questions

1 Interactive Controls: When selecting genes to knock out or overexpress in the UI, does selecting individual genes feel intuitive, or would preset pathway panels (e.g., insulin/IGF-1 signaling) be more useful?
2 Visual Representation: How useful is the visual worm mockup at specific timepoints compared to traditional survival curves? What morphological cues (e.g., movement degradation, lipid accumulation) would make that mockup actionable rather than just decorative?
3 Output Confidence: When the tool computes a change in median lifespan, what diagnostic details (such as rescaled reaction bounds or bottlenecked fluxes) do you need to inspect before trusting that prediction? 
4 Exportability: How would you expect to save or share your simulation runs (e.g., a unique shareable URL, downloadable CSVs of flux distributions, or a PDF summary report)?
